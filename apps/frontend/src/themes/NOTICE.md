# Theme source attribution

Classic retains the original Flexoki color maps. Wealthfolio supplies the
extended financial/chart contract and curated adaptations.

Ember's light card and control surfaces and geometry (radii, shadows) are
adapted from the community theme "Claude +" by Luis Llanes, published on tweakcn
(https://tweakcn.com/themes/cmdght103000n04lh3e2ae93r). tweakcn is licensed
Apache-2.0, Copyright (c) Sahaj Jain. Wealthfolio uses its existing Inter and
JetBrains Mono fonts for this adaptation and a neutral page background.

Terminal is adapted from "Terminal" by Tom Byrne, published on tweakcn
(https://tweakcn.com/themes/cmll14cgf000204ky5ms2fdgj). Wealthfolio retains its
green-on-black dark palette and glow shadows, adds a light appearance, and
adjusts muted/destructive text and chart colors for readability. It uses the
existing JetBrains Mono font and app-specific square geometry/shadow tokens;
VT323 is not bundled.
